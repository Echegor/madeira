Critical Safety Timing Alert

European Daylight Fallback: 17:35 PM Sunset

Clocks in Portugal fall back one hour on Sunday, October 25, 2026.

The Operational Rule

Mandatory 08:30 AM Starts

Because sunset occurs at 17:35 PM, long stages (20–22 km) plus a 1-hour lunch require 7 hours total elapsed time.

  • Depart Trailhead: 08:30 AM – 09:00 AM sharp.
  • Arrive at Hotel: 15:30 PM – 16:00 PM.
  • Safety Margin: 1.5 to 2 full hours of daylight buffer before dusk.
Why This Matters

Zero Night Hiking on Sea Cliffs

The trail frequently traverses narrow cliff paths 60 meters above crashing Atlantic waves. There is no artificial lighting anywhere on the route.

⚠
Strict Guideline: Never hike past 17:15 PM. If delayed, use the emergency taxi dispatch numbers to catch a ride to your lodging.

Saturday Day Trip • October 31 • Silver Coast

Nazaré: World’s Largest Waves & Medieval Óbidos

Witness the colossal Atlantic power of Praia do Norte and explore Portugal’s best-preserved 14th-century walled village.

Big Wave Capital of the World

Praia do Norte & Forte de São Miguel

  • The Submarine Canyon: The 16,000-ft deep underwater Nazaré Canyon funnels massive Atlantic winter swells directly into the clifftop, generating 60- to 80-foot waves that hold all Guinness World Records.
  • The Lighthouse Fort: Walk out onto the cliff promontory to the iconic red lighthouse (Farol da Nazaré) at the 16th-century Forte de São Miguel Arcanjo. You look straight down at the giant waves breaking beneath your feet.
  • Surfer Wall Museum: Inside the fort, see the actual surfboards of big wave legends (Garrett McNamara, Sebastian Steudtner, Maya Gabeira) and wave science exhibits.
  • Easy Day Trip Transit: Direct Rede Expressos coach from Lisbon Sete Rios terminal takes just 1h 40m (~€12/ticket), or a 1h 15m drive via highway A8.
Fairytale Walled Castle (En Route)

Medieval Village of Óbidos

  • 14th-Century Ramparts: Completely enclosed within intact medieval stone walls. Stroll cobblestone alleys flanked by whitewashed houses and bougainvillea.
  • Ginjinha de Óbidos: Taste the famous traditional sour cherry liqueur served in edible dark chocolate cups at local village stalls.
  • Perfect Synergy: Located directly on the A8 corridor between Lisbon and Nazaré—effortlessly paired together in a single Saturday excursion.
  • Return for Evening in Lisbon: Head back to Lisbon by late afternoon for your farewell seafood feast in Chiado.

Local Customs & Clarity

Inclusions & Portuguese Tipping Custom

  • Included (Shared): 7 Nights clean 3★/4★ double rooms with ensuite bath & breakfast; point-to-point daily luggage portage (2 bags); express coach tickets (Lisbon ⇄ Trail); airport metro/taxis.
  • Excluded: À la carte lunches/dinners; Mira River ferry crossing (€5 cash/person); personal hiking gear; travel insurance.
  • Tipping Reality in Portugal: Tipping is NOT customary in Portugal. By law, service is included in all prices, and staff receive regular full wages. There is no US-style percentage tipping (no 15%–20%).
  • Restaurant Gratuities: In casual tascas and cafes, locals either leave nothing or simply leave spare change coins (€1–€2 total) on the table if service was especially friendly.
  • Taxis, Metro & Luggage Portage: Zero tip expected. Rounding up to the nearest euro or leaving 50 cents is polite but strictly optional. Baggage handlers do not expect tips.
