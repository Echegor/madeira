## 1. Traveler Constraints & Rules of Engagement
* **Shared Financials (Zero Per-Person Division):** The travelers share all expenses as a couple. NEVER divide costs per person or present "per person" columns. All figures must be expressed as single, combined totals for both guests.
* **Accommodation Standards:** Comfortable, highly rated 3★ and 4★ hotels, traditional Portuguese quintas, and ocean-view guesthouses (~€80–€120/night). Double room with private en-suite bathroom and breakfast. Zero 5-star palatial luxury or resort fee padding.
* **Zero Alcohol:** The travelers do not drink alcohol. Exclude wine pairings, poncha, and beer. Replace with authentic regional non-alcoholic specialties: freshly squeezed *sumo de maracujá* (passion fruit), *Brisa Maracujá* (local passion fruit sparkling soda), *Água das Pedras* sparkling mineral water, fresh orange juice, and espresso (*bica*).
* **Ground Logistics:** Realistic, practical ground transport: compact/mid-size rental car with good climbing power for mountain roads, or scheduled airport aerobus/coaches. Zero private Mercedes chauffeurs or private yachts.
* **Tipping Culture in Portugal:** Adhere to authentic Portuguese customs. Service is legally included in all prices; waiters earn standard wages; percentage tipping does not exist. Budget only a modest €20–€30 coin round-up buffer for the entire week.
* **Exhaustive Research & High Visual Quality:** Every slide must match the depth, verified logistics, and high-resolution photography of the Fisherman's Trail template. Use verified Unsplash URLs tailored specifically to Madeira (mountains, levadas, waterfalls, ocean pools, traditional villages).

## 2. Master 30-Slide Expedition Blueprint

  * *Eyebrow:* Private Island Expedition • Master Field Guide

  * Contrast the sunny, arid south coast against the lush, mist-draped north coast and high 1,800m volcanic peaks. Core philosophy: early morning alpine starts above cloud inversions, afternoon ocean plunges.

  * Live AllTrails and Wikiloc GPS trail links.
* **Slide 04: Madeira Meteorology & Cloud Inversion Survival**
  * Guide to using the live *Netmadeira* island webcams before driving up to peaks. Understanding trade winds (*Alísios*), Foehn effect, and temperature drops from sea level (22°C) to peaks (8°C).
* **Slide 05: Mountain Driving & Island Transit Architecture**
  * Navigating Madeira's 150+ highway tunnels, the VR1 coastal expressway, 25% steep village grades, parking etiquette at trailheads, and manual vs automatic rental car choice.

  * High-lumen waterproof headlamp (essential for long unlit water tunnels); breathable trail runners (Vibram Megagrip for wet stone); lightweight waterproof hard shell; compact dry bag.

### ACT II: EAST COAST & THE DRAGON'S TAIL (Slides 8–13)
* **Slide 08: Day 1: Arrival at FNC & Historic Funchal Base**
  * Touchdown at Cristiano Ronaldo Airport (FNC); scenic coastal drive into Funchal; orientation walk through the historic *Zona Velha* cobblestones and painted doors (*Rua de Santa Maria*).

  * Authentic dinner at a traditional *churrascaria* (e.g., *Restaurante Santo António* in Estreito de Câmara de Lobos). Tender beef skewers hung over tables, garlic butter flatbread, and fresh passion fruit juice.

* **Slide 12: Day 2 Coast: Caniçal Harbor & Prainha Volcanic Beach**

* **Slide 13: Night 2 Lodging & East Coast Dining**
  * Relaxed evening on the east coast (Machico / Caniçal) with fresh grilled swordfish (*espada*) paired with local bananas and tropical salad.

### ACT III: ALPINE PEAKS & EMERALD LEVADAS (Slides 14–19)
* **Slide 14: Day 3: PR1 Pico do Arieiro to Pico Ruivo (The Master Ridge)**

  * 12 km round-trip flat levada hike deep into the UNESCO Laurissilva rainforest. Passing through four hand-carved rock tunnels to a roaring 100-meter emerald waterfall amphitheater.
* **Slide 18: Day 4 Heritage: Santana Thatched Cottages & Mountain Stew**
  * Visiting the iconic triangular thatched houses (*Palheiros*) of Santana, followed by a hearty lunch of Madeiran tomato and onion soup with poached eggs.
* **Slide 19: Night 4 Lodging: North Coast Ocean View Inn**
  * Oceanfront 3★/4★ stay in São Vicente or Ponta Delgada, falling asleep to the sound of powerful northern Atlantic surf.
